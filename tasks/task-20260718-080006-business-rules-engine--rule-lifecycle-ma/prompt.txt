Findings to address:
- [High] Business Rule Engine Completeness
  Gap: A generic reusable Business Rules Engine (author/store/evaluate arbitrary conditional rules) does not exist as a product capability
  Recommended approach: Design a generic rule schema (condition tree + action) reusing approvalWorkflowStepDefinitions' conditionField/Operator/Value precedent, exposed to org admins.
- [High] Business Rule Versioning
  Gap: Business Rule Versioning does not exist -- no mechanism tracks prior versions of a rule or lets an org roll back
  Recommended approach: Prerequisite: build the engine in row 4 first, with a version column and change history from day one.
- [Medium] Business Rule Testing Framework
  Gap: No Business Rule Testing Framework (dry-run a rule against sample data before activating it) exists
  Recommended approach: Build a 'test against sample record' mode once the rule engine (row 4) exists.
- [Low] Business Rule Simulation
  Gap: Business Rule Simulation (what-if impact analysis before activating a rule) does not exist
  Recommended approach: Lower priority than versioning/testing; revisit after the base engine and testing framework ship.
