## KNOWN_CONTEXT
- scripts/prompt_gateway/gateway.py (built task-20260725-053025, merged PR #60) was designed and tested against short, single-instruction chat-style text (its own real test fixtures were sentences, not documents). It has never been run against a real multi-page structured document before this task. Expect the first run(s) to reveal it does not handle document-scale input well at all -- likely candidates: ChatClassifier forces one category/one intent onto the whole document regardless of length or internal structure, entity extraction (engine/classifier.py) was tuned for short strings and may saturate or garble on long text, PromptEngine's noise-removal/template-conversion logic assumes a single imperative sentence. This may require real feature work (e.g. a document-mode / section-chunking path through the pipeline that processes the document's real structure section-by-section rather than forcing it through the single-message path), not just regex tuning -- read scripts/prompt_gateway/engine/*.py in full before assuming small tweaks will be enough.
- Extract the document's real text first (python-docx or an equivalent real library -- check what's already available on this server/in this repo before installing anything new) -- do not paraphrase or hand-summarize the document yourself as a substitute for real extraction.
- "Gap" and "accuracy" must be a real, defensible, numeric measurement you define and document precisely (e.g. a structured content-coverage score: what fraction of the document's real distinct headings/sections/key entities/requirements are recoverable from OWNER_ENGINE's output prompt versus a direct reading of the source) -- do not report a vibes-based percentage. State your exact measurement method in the final checkpoint so it's reproducible.
- The Owner's own bar is explicit and non-negotiable: this task is not complete until the real measured gap is below 5% (real accuracy >=95%). The RUN 1 through RUN 10 structure the Owner described is the iteration mechanism, not a hard stop -- if 10 iterations aren't enough to reach the real bar, keep iterating (the Owner's own words: "TAKE OWN DECISION to ensure the gap...decreases to less than 5%...FOR THIS AS AI YOU HAVE ALL THE PERMISSIONS"). If after a genuinely large, good-faith number of iterations the bar still cannot be reached (e.g. a fundamental architectural limit you've identified and can name precisely), report that honestly with the real evidence and the real blocking reason -- never round up or claim 95% when the real number is lower.
