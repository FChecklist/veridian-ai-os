VERIDIAN Review Framework gap-closure: Checks & Balances / Business Rule & Calculation Verification.
This covers 6 related finding(s) from the framework evaluation. Close all of them in one coherent PR if they share the same module/area -- do not create a separate PR per finding if they're naturally one piece of work.

Findings to address:
- [Medium] Business Rule Validation Before Execution
  Gap: Validation covers structural/numeric sanity, not a full 'does this violate any business rule' pre-execution check, since no generic Business Rules Engine exists (see row 4)
  Recommended approach: Once a rule engine exists, wire it as a pre-execution gate alongside dispatch-output-validator.ts.
- [Medium] Calculation Cross-Verification
  Gap: Calculation Cross-Verification (two different methods/engines agreeing before a result is trusted) is not built as a systematic practice
  Recommended approach: For financially material calculations (GST, payroll, EMI), add a second independent computation path or a sanity-bound check.
- [Medium] AI Output Validation by Business Rules
  Gap: This validates request eligibility, not the CONTENT of AI-generated output against business rules after generation (e.g. checking an AI-drafted number against a business rule)
  Recommended approach: Add a post-generation validation pass for AI outputs feeding into business-critical fields, reusing the row-4 rule engine once built.
